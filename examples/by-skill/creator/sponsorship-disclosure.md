# Sponsorship Disclosure

`sponsorship-disclosure`

## What this is for

Write a clear sponsorship disclosure for the placement the creator is actually publishing.

## Scenario

Maya is posting the Hearth Pan Reel on 18 September 2026. She was paid $400 and kept the pan. The draft caption starts with 'so grateful'. She needs the disclosure line that goes above the recommendation.

## Example data

```text
value received: $400 fee plus the skillet, retail $89, kept
placement: Instagram Reel, 18 September 2026, plus 3 stories the same day
platform label she was told to use: Paid partnership
affiliate link: no
caption draft: starts with "so grateful"
```

## Example outcome

**Disclosure, above the recommendation**

Paid partnership. Hearth Pan paid me $400 and gave me the skillet to keep.

**Stories**
Same sentence on frame 1 of each story, not only on the last frame.

**Do not use**
"So grateful" as the only label. A #gifted tag with no fee. A disclosure under the hashtags.

This is the line for this placement. It is not a legal opinion.
