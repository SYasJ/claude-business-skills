`short-story-draft`

## What this is for

Draft a short story with a clear arc — setup, tension, and a resolution that earns its ending.

**How this draft was built**

**1. Establish the character and the want in the first paragraph. A story that spends three paragraphs on setting before introducing a person loses the reader**

**2. Introduce the obstacle in a way that raises the stakes. The obstacle must matter to the character**

**3. Build tension through a decision the character must make. A story where things just happen to a passive character is not a story**

**4. Write the resolution in proportion to the tension. A two-page climax earns a half-page ending. A short build does not earn a long reflection**

**5. Use specific, concrete details. A 'small house in a suburb' is weaker than 'a three-room rental above a dry cleaner on Elbow Drive'**

**Deliberately not done**
- A passive main character who observes but never decides.
- A twist that contradicts established facts.
- Real people placed in harmful fictional situations.
