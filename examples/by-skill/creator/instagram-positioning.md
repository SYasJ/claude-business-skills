# Instagram Positioning

`instagram-positioning`

## What this is for

Position an Instagram account around the grid people already see, not a niche the creator wishes they had.

## Scenario

Maya's grid is 18 dinner photos and 2 cafe photos. She wants the bio to say 'wellness and travel'. The dinners are the grid.

## Example data

```text
grid: 18 dinners, 2 cafes, last 20 posts
bio draft: wellness and travel
what people save: dinner lists, she checked 6 saves, all dinner posts
city: Calgary
will not add: travel as a promise, she has one cafe post from Banff in July
```

## Example outcome

**Position**
Bio: Weeknight dinners in Calgary. 30 minutes. The list is on the post.

**Grid matches**
Dinners. The two cafe frames stay as life, not as a travel niche.

**Bio does not say**
Wellness. Travel. She has one Banff frame and no wellness proof.

**Highlights**
Lists. Clock. Grocer. Not 'wellness'.
