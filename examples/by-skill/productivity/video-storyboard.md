# Video Storyboard

`video-storyboard`

## What this is for

Write a shot-by-shot visual storyboard for a video, naming what is on screen, the motion, and the audio for each shot.

---

## Scenario A — 60-second explainer: "How compound interest works"

**Creator:** Samir Qureshi, personal finance channel, 3 scenes requested, ~60 seconds

### Storyboard

| Shot | Duration | On-screen | Motion | VO / Audio |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 — Title card | 3s | Bold text: **"Your $1,000 becomes $4,000 — without doing anything"** on dark background with subtle coin animation | Coins fade in from bottom | No VO. Upbeat background music begins. |
| 2 — The problem | 10s | Split screen: left = piggy bank (static savings, no growth); right = nothing | Left half fades in, then right half fades in as contrast | VO: "Most people leave money in a savings account earning almost nothing. But there's a way to make your money earn money." |
| 3 — Formula intro | 14s | Screen recording: simple spreadsheet. Year 1: $1,000. Year 2: $1,050. Year 3: $1,102.50. Formula cell visible. Text overlay: **"5% annual interest"** | Rows animate in one by one as VO speaks | VO: "If you invest $1,000 at 5%, after year one you have $1,050. Year two, you earn interest on that $1,050 — not just the original thousand. That's compounding." |
| 4 — Growth chart | 12s | Animated bar chart: 10 years of growth from $1,000 to ~$1,629. X-axis: years. Y-axis: dollar value. No exact prediction language. | Bars grow left to right, animating in real-time | VO: "Over 10 years, that $1,000 grows to over $1,600 — without you adding a single dollar. Start earlier and the effect multiplies." |
| 5 — Three examples | 12s | Three cards side by side: (A) $1K for 10 yr → ~$1,629 / (B) $5K for 10 yr → ~$8,144 / (C) $1K for 30 yr → ~$4,322. Disclaimer: "Illustrative only. Past rates vary." | Cards slide in from right | VO: "Five thousand dollars for ten years becomes over eight thousand. One thousand dollars for thirty years becomes over four thousand. The variable that matters most isn't the amount — it's time." |
| 6 — CTA | 9s | Screen: **"Subscribe for more money basics"** + channel logo. Subscribe button animation. | Logo fades in, subscribe button pulses | VO: "Hit subscribe for more plain-English money explainers every week." Music fades. |

**Total: ~60 seconds.** All figures are illustrative; add a screen disclaimer and a pinned comment with a sources note.

### Footage you need

| Shot | What to source |
|---|---|
| Shot 1 | Stock animation of coins, or use a free motion graphic from Motion Array |
| Shot 2 | Piggy bank graphic — royalty-free or your own illustration |
| Shot 3 | Screen recording of your own spreadsheet (do not use someone else's video) |
| Shot 4 | Animated chart — create in Canva, Flourish, or After Effects |
| Shot 5 | Three cards — design in Canva; use a template |
| Shot 6 | Your channel logo + subscribe button animation |

## Scenario B — 90-second product demo video: SaaS onboarding

**Creator:** marketing team, Fieldnote SaaS, audience = new trial users

| Shot | Duration | On-screen | Motion | VO / Audio |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 — Hook | 5s | Text: **"Set up your first project in under 2 minutes"** | White text on brand color background, animates in | VO: "Here's everything you need to get started." |
| 2 — Dashboard | 15s | Screen recording: logged-in dashboard. Cursor moves to "New Project" button. | Live screen recording, cursor highlight | VO: "From the dashboard, click New Project. Name it, pick a template, and click Create." |
| 3 — Invite team | 12s | Screen recording: People tab → Add Member → type an email → Send invite. | Same screen recording flow | VO: "Add your team from the People tab. They'll get an email invite and can join immediately." |
| 4 — First task | 14s | Screen recording: task list → Add Task → type title → assign to person → set due date. | Screen recording | VO: "Add your first task. Assign it to someone on your team and set a due date. Fieldnote will notify them automatically." |
| 5 — Key features callout | 20s | Split screen: three panels (Kanban view / Timeline view / Reporting). Each panel highlighted briefly. | Panels slide in | VO: "Switch between Kanban, Timeline, or Reporting view — whatever fits how your team thinks. No extra setup required." |
| 6 — CTA | 10s | Text: **"Your free trial is waiting"** + URL: fieldnote.io/trial. Subscribe/sign-up animation. | Fade in | VO: "Your 14-day free trial is waiting. No credit card needed. Link in the description." |
| 7 — Outro | 14s | Branded end card with social links. | Static | Outro music. |
