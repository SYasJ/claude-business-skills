# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

This project exists so that people doing real work — accountants, dispatchers,
engineers, HR coordinators — can get consistent help from an agent. That only
works if people from all backgrounds feel able to say "this is not how the job
actually goes."

We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Saying what you actually do in practice, especially when it contradicts a skill
- Giving domain reasoning, not just a verdict
- Accepting that someone with ten years in a field may be right and you may not be
- Focusing on what is best for the people using these skills

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing someone's private information without explicit permission
- Dismissing a contributor's domain experience because of their background

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and
when someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer through a
[GitHub issue](https://github.com/SYasJ/claude-business-skills/issues) or, for
anything sensitive, by private message. Reports are reviewed and investigated
promptly and fairly. The maintainer will respect the privacy and security of
whoever reports an incident.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may face
temporary or permanent consequences.
